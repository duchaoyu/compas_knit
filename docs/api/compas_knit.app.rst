compas_knit.app
********************************************************************************

.. currentmodule:: compas_knit.app

.. rst-class:: lead

A window to simulate a knit interactively, and export the settings of the simulation, the stitch, the stretch factors,
the material and the loads, for the fabrication: ``scripts/sim/sim_window.py`` opens it, and
``scripts/fab/5_pattern_from_settings.py`` makes the knitting pattern from the settings.
